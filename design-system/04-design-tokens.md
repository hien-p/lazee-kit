## Typography

Display:

- Baloo 2, Fredoka, or Nunito Sans
- Use for hero, slide titles, mascot labels, large CTAs

UI:

- Inter, Geist, or Satoshi
- Use for proposal text, forms, dashboards, technical cards

Mono:

- JetBrains Mono or SF Mono
- Use for addresses, method names, contract IDs, receipts

## Type Scale

| Role | Desktop | Mobile | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hero | 64-88px | 40-48px | 800 |
| Page H1 | 48-56px | 34-40px | 800 |
| Section H2 | 32-40px | 28px | 750 |
| Card title | 20-24px | 18-22px | 750 |
| Body | 16-18px | 16px | 400-500 |
| Small | 13-14px | 13px | 500 |
| Badge | 11-12px | 11px | 700 |

Letter spacing should stay at 0.

## Radius

- Tiny controls: 8px
- Inputs: 14px
- Cards: 20px
- Hero panels: 28px
- Mascot containers: 32-48px
- Sticker badges: 999px or custom blob

## Shadows

- Soft sticker: `0 14px 40px rgba(46, 46, 46, 0.12)`
- Floating mascot: `0 24px 70px rgba(255, 111, 163, 0.22)`
- Panel: `0 10px 24px rgba(46, 46, 46, 0.08)`
- Protocol card: `0 1px 0 rgba(46, 46, 46, 0.08)`
